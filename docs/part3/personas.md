# Part 3 personas: York University Station ventilation and air quality upgrade

Five fictional, composite personas built from the group's stakeholder interviews. These summaries, and nothing from the interview notes, are given to the AI agent with the project description. The interview log and anonymised notes are kept in the group's York drive and the report appendices, not in this repo.

---

Persona summaries (fictional composites from our stakeholder interviews; names are invented):

Persona 1: Sara
Identity: York University student who commutes to campus by subway
Based on: Interview 1
Needs:
- A comfortable, breathable platform, especially during early morning and evening commutes
- Clear, on-time updates about construction progress, service disruptions and reopening schedules
- Alternative transportation that keeps delays and inconvenience low during construction
Worries:
- York University Station being closed or out of service during construction
- Longer commutes by bus, and having to leave earlier to get to class on time
- Other students and staff facing the same transportation problems

Persona 2: Marcus
Identity: University staff member who takes the subway to work
Based on: Interviews 2 and 4
Needs:
- Platform air that feels fresh, not stale
- A construction plan that keeps the platform open and easy to walk through
- A clear plan for looking after the new system after the project ends
Worries:
- Noise, dust and blocked areas on the platform during construction
- The project going over budget and over time
- The new system being installed and then forgotten
- Losing platform space and having to change his route through the station

Persona 3: Jordan
Identity: York University undergraduate who commutes on Line 1 from downtown
Based on: Interview 3
Needs:
- Fresher air on the platform during longer midday waits between classes
- Same-day notices when platform areas, escalators or elevators are affected by construction
- A ventilation system that ramps up for class-change crowds, not only rush hour
- Fan noise kept tolerable during quieter parts of the day
Worries:
- Construction blocking escalators or elevators and forcing long detours with a heavy bag
- Dust and noise making midday waits worse during the install
- New fans being loud once the system opens
- Sensors tuned only to morning and evening peaks and ignoring midday crowding
- Work overrunning into exam periods

Persona 4: Noah
Identity: York University student who commutes from Vaughan on Line 1
Based on: Interviews 4 and 2
Needs:
- Platform air that is comfortable to breathe, especially during rush hour and on hot summer days
- Clear information about what the new sensors collect: that they only count people and do not record faces or track phones
- A ventilation system that still works properly in an emergency and does not interfere with the station's smoke exhaust system
- A screen or sign on the platform showing air quality, so riders can see the upgrade is making a difference
Worries:
- Construction dust making the air worse for months before it gets better
- Passenger-counting sensors turning out to be cameras that collect riders' data without telling them
- The TTC spending $3 million and riders not being able to tell anything changed

Persona 5: Priya
Identity: University student who regularly commutes to campus using the TTC
Based on: Interview 5
Needs:
- Comfortable temperatures and better air circulation on the platform
- Construction work outside busy university hours whenever possible
- Reliable subway service with minimal interruptions
- An energy-efficient ventilation system that is regularly maintained
- Noticeable improvements in air quality throughout the year
Worries:
- Construction delays affecting their ability to get to classes on time
- Noise, dust and restricted platform access during construction
- Increased energy use from the new equipment
- The project going over its budget and timeline
- The system becoming less effective because of poor maintenance
